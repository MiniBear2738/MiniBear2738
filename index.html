👑 Tiny Kingdom

A cozy little settlement that mostly runs itself. Villagers chop trees, mine rocks, haul everything back to the storage barn and construct new buildings. Your job: nudge it along.

How to play

  • Click trees & rocks to gather a resource yourself, or click a villager to say hi. Hover a villager to see their name and what they're up to.
  • Pick a building from the toolbar, then click the ground to place a construction site — villagers will build it.
  • Houses raise the population cap; lumber camps / quarries speed up gathering; farms 🌾 grow food.
  • Food is eaten a little each day. If it runs out villagers don't starve — they just get hungry and slow, so keep a farm or two going.
  • Plant trees & rocks anywhere, and click-drag to paint whole forests. Drag a path 🛤️ (1🪨 each) to give villagers a 20% speed boost.
  • Villagers specialise: chop or mine enough and they become a 🪓 Lumberjack or ⛏️ Miner, working their trade 40% faster.
  • Seasons drift by — 🌸 spring, ☀️ summer, 🍂 autumn, ❄️ winter — recolouring the world. Crops race in summer; nature regrows slowly under snow.
  • Auto-grow lets villagers plan and fund new buildings themselves — turn it off for full control.
  • Watch for events: 🌧️ rain slows gathering but regrows nature fast, 🌾 harvest speeds gathering, 🎊 celebrations make villagers quick.
